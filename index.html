Method Summary

The dashboard estimates the profitability threshold X0, which is the minimum annual gasoline volume required for a service station to make the VRRefiner investment profitable within the selected payback period.

Net fuel value (%) adjusts the effective value of recovered gasoline after taxes, margins or other deductions. For example, if the pump price is 1.60 €/L and net fuel value is 75%, the model uses 1.20 €/L.

Countries are colored according to their estimated market potential: green for highly attractive, yellow for moderate, red for low potential and grey for countries without data.
